## 8. The Unit Economics

### Per Job Economics

```
Average job value: £1,100 (EV), £5,000 (heat pump)
Platform take (8%): £88 (EV), £400 (heat pump)
AI receptionist cost: £5
Payment processing: £30 (2.7%)
Support cost: £10
Net per job: £43 (EV), £365 (heat pump)

Dispute risk: £1,000 per 10 jobs = £100/job
Risk-adjusted net: depends on dispute rate
```

### Break-Even Analysis

```
Monthly fixed costs: £2,000
  AI infrastructure: £500
  Support: £500
  Development: £500
  Marketing: £500

At 2% dispute rate: £2,000 / (£43 - £20) = 87 jobs/month
At 1% dispute rate: £2,000 / (£43 - £10) = 61 jobs/month
```

**Key insight:** Dispute rate must be <3% for the model to work.
