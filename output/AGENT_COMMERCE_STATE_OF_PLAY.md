## 1. The Middleman That Ties Everything Together

The most compelling campaign isn't a single product store. It's a **progressive relationship** with tradesmen that starts with lead generation and evolves into full operational support.

### The Evolution

```
Stage 1: LEAD GENERATION
    EV charger installation leads
    ↓
Stage 2: BOOKING PLATFORM
    AI agent books appointments
    ↓
Stage 3: OPERATIONAL SUPPORT
    AI receptionist handles calls, scheduling
    ↓
Stage 4: PARTS & SUPPLY
    Recommend parts, affiliate deals
    ↓
Stage 5: FULL OPERATIONS
    Tax, invoicing, CRM, reviews
    ↓
Stage 6: SUPPLIER LOCK-IN
    App knows when parts are low
    Tradie never leaves
```

### Why This Works

The contractor gets:
- AI front desk (missed calls, booking, quotes)
- Parts recommendations (affiliate revenue for us)
- Tax/invoicing help
- Review management
- Customer database

We get:
- Live availability data
- Service area data
- Pricing data
- Quality scores
- Completion data
- Repeat customer data

**The flywheel:**
```
more contractor usage → better supply graph → better agent answers
→ more bookings → more contractor demand → more contractor usage
```

## 7. The Unit Economics

### Per Job Economics

```
Average job value: £1,100
Platform take (8%): £88
AI receptionist cost: £5 (amortized)
Payment processing: £30 (2.7%)
Support cost: £10
Net per job: £43

Dispute risk: £1,000 per 10 jobs = £100/job
Risk-adjusted net: £43 - £100 = -£57 per job (if dispute rate is 10%)
```

**Key insight:** Dispute risk dominates. The platform must have:
- Installation evidence (photos, timestamps)
- Clear scope/change-order rules
- Provider liability insurance
- Payment architecture (Stripe Connect)

### Break-Even Analysis

```
Monthly fixed costs: £2,000
  AI infrastructure: £500
  Support: £500
  Development: £500
  Marketing: £500

Break-even jobs: £2,000 / £43 = 47 jobs/month

At 8% take rate on £1,100 average:
  Revenue needed: £2,000 / 0.08 = £25,000/month
  Jobs needed: £25,000 / £1,100 = 23 jobs/month
```

**With risk adjustment:**
```
At 5% dispute rate: £2,000 / (£43 - £50) = negative (unprofitable)
At 2% dispute rate: £2,000 / (£43 - £20) = 87 jobs/month
At 1% dispute rate: £2,000 / (£43 - £10) = 61 jobs/month
```

**Key insight:** Dispute rate must be <3% for the model to work.

## 8. The Risk Mitigation

### Installation Evidence

Every job must capture:
- Pre-installation photos
- Post-installation photos
- GPS location
- Timestamp
- Customer signature
- Provider signature

### Scope Management

- Clear quote with itemized costs
- Change-order process
- Customer approval required
- Provider approval required

### Payment Architecture

- Stripe Connect for split payments
- Delayed payout until completion
- Dispute resolution process
- Provider liability insurance

### Insurance

- Professional indemnity for providers
- Public liability for platform
- Product liability for parts
