## 1. The Thesis

> **There exists a class of services where high ticket value meets fragmented suppliers meets phone-tag friction. The winning strategy is to own the provider relationship, not just the lead.**

### Why This Works

The modern customer journey for local services:

```
"I need an EV charger installed"
    ↓
Google search
    ↓
10 results: generic directories, manufacturer sites, forums
    ↓
None answer: "Who can install MY charger in MY postcode?"
    ↓
Customer calls, contractor misses call
    ↓
Customer calls another, gets quote, books
    ↓
First contractor loses the job
```

The opportunity:

```
"I need an EV charger installed"
    ↓
Google search
    ↓
Specialist site: "Tell us what charger you have"
    ↓
AI compatibility checker
    ↓
3 OZEV-approved installers with availability
    ↓
Automated booking
    ↓
Contractor gets job via AI receptionist
```
