# GeoDrop Hypotheses

*The thesis is geographic arbitrage. These are the falsifiable hypotheses within it. Each store is an experiment. Data proves or disproves. If proven, the insight transfers to the next store.*

---

## The Structure

```
THESIS: Geographic arbitrage is the competitive advantage
  │
  ├── H1: Language Localization Converts
  ├── H2: Specialist Trust Beats Generic Selection
  ├── H3: Free Listings Validate Before Paid
  ├── H4: EU Distribution Enables Cross-Border
  ├── H5: High Ticket Solves Small Budgets
  ├── H6: The Good Seller Gap Predicts Success
  ├── H7: Content Gap ≠ Commerce Gap
  ├── H8: One Country First Beats Multi-Country
  ├── H9: Operations Are The Post-Validation Bottleneck
  └── H10: Replication Compounds Learning
```

## H1: Language Localization Converts

**Statement:** Stores with native-language content convert 2x+ better than English-only stores in the same market.

**Falsifier:** If a Norwegian-language store converts at <1.5x the rate of an English-only store selling the same products to the same audience.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Conversion rate (CVR)
- Norwegian store: >1.5% CVR
- English control: <1% CVR
- Sample size: 100+ clicks each
- Window: 30 days

**Experiment:**
1. Launch same 20 products in Norwegian (moltwork.com/davis)
2. Track CVR for Norwegian pages vs English pages
3. Compare after 100+ clicks per variant

**If Proven:** Build native-language content for every new country. Language is a moat.

**If Disproven:** Language doesn't matter as much as product selection. Focus on product × economics instead.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every non-English market.

## H2: Specialist Trust Beats Generic Selection

**Statement:** A store that positions as "Davis weather station specialist" converts better than a store that sells "weather stations + marine equipment + electronics."

**Falsifier:** If a multi-category store converts at ≥1.5x the rate of a specialist store for the same products.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Conversion rate (CVR) + AOV
- Specialist store: >1.5% CVR, AOV >NOK 15,000
- Generic control: <1% CVR, AOV <NOK 10,000
- Sample size: 50+ clicks each
- Window: 30 days

**Experiment:**
1. Position moltwork.com/davis as specialist (comparison pages, guides, expert content)
2. Track conversion behavior vs generic marine stores (BestMarin, Dalebakken)
3. Monitor bounce rate, time on site, pages per session

**If Proven:** Niche down further. Build specialist stores, not general stores.

**If Disproven:** Broad catalogs win. Product range matters more than positioning.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every specialist store.

## H3: Free Listings Validate Before Paid

**Statement:** Free listings generate enough signal (impressions, clicks, CTR) to validate a product-market fit before spending on ads.

**Falsifier:** If free listings produce <10 impressions/day after 14 days with correct feed and content.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Impressions, clicks, CTR
- Free listings: >100 impressions/day by day 14
- CTR: >2%
- Clicks: >10/day by day 14
- Window: 14 days

**Experiment:**
1. Upload 20 products to Merchant Center
2. Activate free listings
3. Monitor Search Console daily
4. Do NOT run any paid ads for 14 days

**If Proven:** Always start with free listings. $0 validation works.

**If Disproven:** Free listings are too slow. Need paid to get signal fast.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every new store launch.

---

## H4: EU Distribution Enables Cross-Border

**Statement:** DistriHUB (or similar EU distributor) can fulfill orders to any Nordic country within 5 business days, making cross-border dropship viable.

**Falsifier:** If average delivery time from DistriHUB to Finland/Norway exceeds 7 business days for 80%+ of orders.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Delivery time, delivery success rate
- Delivery: <5 business days for 80%+ of orders
- Success rate: >95% (no lost/damaged packages)
- Sample size: 20+ orders
- Window: 30 days

**Experiment:**
1. Get DistriHUB approval
2. Place 20 test orders to Finnish/Norwegian addresses
3. Track delivery time from order to doorstep
4. Measure customer satisfaction

**If Proven:** Cross-border EU dropship works. Scale to all Nordic countries.

**If Disproven:** Cross-border logistics are unreliable. Need local warehousing.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every EU cross-border store.

## H5: High Ticket Solves Small Budgets

**Statement:** Products with AOV >NOK 5,000 can be profitably tested at $5-10/day, while products with AOV <NOK 1,000 cannot.

**Falsifier:** If a low-AOV product (<NOK 1,000) achieves positive contribution profit at $10/day within 30 days.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Contribution profit per click
- High AOV (>NOK 5,000): positive contribution within 30 days at $5-10/day
- Low AOV (<NOK 1,000): negative contribution at $10/day after 30 days
- Sample size: 100+ clicks each
- Window: 30 days

**Experiment:**
1. Test Davis weather stations (AOV NOK 15,000+) at $5/day
2. Test robot vacuums (AOV NOK 5,000) at $5/day
3. Compare contribution profit per click after 30 days

**If Proven:** Always prioritize high-AOV products for small-budget testing.

**If Disproven:** Low-AOV can work with enough volume. AOV isn't the deciding factor.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every product selection decision.

## H6: The Good Seller Gap Predicts Success

**Statement:** Products with high demand but few GOOD sellers (not few total sellers) are the strongest opportunities.

**Falsifier:** If a product with many total sellers but few good sellers performs worse than a product with few total sellers but many good sellers.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Revenue per click, conversion rate
- High gap (popular + few good sellers): >NOK 5 revenue/click
- Low gap (popular + many good sellers): <NOK 2 revenue/click
- Sample size: 200+ clicks each
- Window: 30 days

**Experiment:**
1. Score 10 products on GOOD_SELLER_GAP (demand × 1/(1+good_sellers))
2. Launch top 3 high-gap products
3. Launch bottom 3 low-gap products
4. Compare revenue per click after 30 days

**If Proven:** The GOOD_SELLER_GAP metric is the primary selection criterion.

**If Disproven:** Other factors (price, brand, seasonality) matter more than seller quality.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every product × country selection.

---

## H7: Content Gap ≠ Commerce Gap

**Statement:** Products with high content gaps (unanswered buyer questions) but low merchant gaps (strong sellers exist) are content opportunities, not store opportunities.

**Falsifier:** If a product with high content gap but low merchant gap generates >NOK 1,000/month in store revenue.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Store revenue from content-gap products
- Content gap + merchant gap: >NOK 1,000/month
- Content gap + NO merchant gap: <NOK 100/month
- Sample size: 1,000+ impressions
- Window: 60 days

**Experiment:**
1. Identify products with high content gap but low merchant gap (e.g., Bosch scanners)
2. Build comparison pages for these products
3. Track if traffic converts to sales vs just pageviews

**If Proven:** Separate content opportunities from store opportunities. Build affiliate content for content gaps, stores for merchant gaps.

**If Disproven:** Content gaps can become store opportunities with enough content investment.

**Transferability:** HIGH — prevents wasting store resources on content-only opportunities.

---

## H8: One Country First Beats Multi-Country

**Statement:** Focusing on one country produces better learning and faster profitability than spreading across 2-3 countries simultaneously.

**Falsifier:** If a multi-country launch (2 countries) achieves profitability faster than a single-country launch.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Time to first profitable sale, total learning (insights generated)
- Single country: profitable within 60 days, 10+ insights
- Multi-country: NOT profitable within 60 days, <5 insights per country
- Budget: same $10/day total

**Experiment:**
1. Launch Norway-only store with $10/day
2. Compare with hypothetical Finland+Norway split ($5/day each)
3. Measure time to profitability and learning rate

**If Proven:** Always launch one country first. Concentrate learning.

**If Disproven:** Multi-country testing accelerates learning through comparison.

**Transferability:** HIGH — applies to every launch decision.

---

## H9: Operations Are The Post-Validation Bottleneck

**Statement:** After a product validates (positive contribution profit), operational issues (supplier reliability, fulfillment, payment) become the primary failure mode.

**Falsifier:** If >50% of post-validation failures are caused by ad/campaign issues rather than operational issues.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Failure mode classification
- Post-validation failures: >50% operational (supplier, shipping, payment)
- Post-validation failures: <50% ad-related (CPC, CVR, budget)
- Sample size: 10+ post-validation failures
- Window: 90 days

**Experiment:**
1. Track every failure after a product validates
2. Classify as: operational, ad-related, market-related, or other
3. Calculate percentage in each category

**If Proven:** After validation, shift focus from ads to operations. Build supplier relationships, fulfillment reliability, payment processing.

**If Disproven:** Ad optimization remains the primary lever even after validation.

**Transferability:** HIGH — determines where to invest resources post-validation.

---

## H10: Replication Compounds Learning

**Statement:** Each successful store generates reusable insights that make the next store faster and cheaper to launch.

**Falsifier:** If the 5th store takes longer to launch than the 1st store.

**Data Target:**
- Metric: Time to launch, cost to launch, time to profitability
- Store 1: 30 days to launch, $500 cost, 60 days to profitability
- Store 5: <15 days to launch, <$200 cost, <30 days to profitability
- Window: 6 months

**Experiment:**
1. Document every process, template, supplier relationship from Store 1
2. Apply to Store 2, 3, 4, 5
3. Measure time and cost reduction

**If Proven:** The system compounds. Each store makes the next one easier. Build the playbook.

**If Disproven:** Each market is unique. Learning doesn't transfer. Start fresh each time.

**Transferability:** CRITICAL — determines whether this is a scalable business or a one-off.

## How These Hypotheses Work Together

```
IF H1 (language converts) AND H2 (specialist beats generic)
  → Build native-language specialist stores

IF H3 (free listings validate) AND H5 (high ticket solves small budgets)
  → Launch with free listings, high-AOV products, $0 spend

IF H6 (good seller gap predicts) AND H7 (content ≠ commerce)
  → Select products using GOOD_SELLER_GAP, not content gap

IF H4 (EU distribution works) AND H8 (one country first)
  → Start with one country, prove the model, then expand

IF H9 (operations are bottleneck) AND H10 (replication compounds)
  → After validation, shift to operations. Build the playbook.

ALL TOGETHER:
  → GeoDrop is validated. Build the geographic intelligence platform.
```
