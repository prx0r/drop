# Evidence scoring

Grades are about usefulness/provenance, not whether the business claim is certainly true.

- **A+** — official platform/API/dataset documentation.
- **A / A-** — unusually granular experiment or accounting source with reproducible structure; may still be self-reported.
- **B+ / B / B-** — useful operator/agency case with chronology or numbers but meaningful commercial incentive / incomplete independent verification.
- **C+ / C** — anecdotal community report; useful as hypothesis/negative evidence, not ground truth.
- **?** — unresolved lead retained so it is not lost.

For model training, keep source grade as a feature and never mix self-reported revenue with audited/official data without provenance.
