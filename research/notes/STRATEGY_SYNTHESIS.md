# Working strategy synthesized from the corpus

## Core loop

1. **Search-demand first for Google-first stores.** Use Merchant Center Best Sellers, Keyword Planner and Trends to find existing buyer intent.
2. **Join demand to economics.** Supplier landed cost + expected fees/returns + price benchmark + CPC + expected CVR. Optimize contribution profit per click, not headline ROAS.
3. **Launch one market and a lean catalog.** Avoid spreading tiny budgets across countries/SKUs.
4. **Exploit free surfaces before paid scale.** Google free listings, Bing Product Listings, Pinterest Product Pins and commercial-intent SEO.
5. **Use $0-$10/day only as a probe.** Prefer exact-model/high-intent Shopping/Search where one high-ticket conversion can finance weeks of testing.
6. **Treat the product feed as an experimental surface.** Titles, GTINs, categories, images and prices should be logged and tested at SKU level.
7. **Observe more often than you act.** Pre-define checkpoints; sparse early data is noisy. Avoid strategy thrash.
8. **Graduate automation carefully.** PMax/value bidding is more credible after clean conversion data; use brand exclusions and margin/value guardrails.
9. **Scale gradually once contribution profit is real.** ~20% budget increments recur as a heuristic; do not treat it as universal law.
10. **Reconcile to store-side P&L.** COGS, shipping, fees, refunds, chargebacks, software and contribution profit matter more than platform ROAS.
11. **After validation, operations become the bottleneck.** Supplier reliability, fulfillment, payment processing and customer support determine whether a winner survives.
12. **For novel impulse products, use demand creation first.** Meta/TikTok/creator discovery may precede Google capture; do not force Google-first onto products nobody searches for.

## Tiny-budget decision rule

Expected contribution profit per click ≈ `expected_CVR * pre_ad_contribution_per_order - CPC`.

A £500 product with £250 pre-ad contribution can survive a much higher CPC than a £25 product with £10 contribution. This is why high-ticket searched products recur in the $0-$10/day case studies.

## Recommended bootstrap

`market data -> supplier match -> specialist store -> free listings/SEO -> free traffic signals -> one $5-$10/day Google test -> first profitable sale -> reinvest -> scale winner -> add adjacent SKUs`
