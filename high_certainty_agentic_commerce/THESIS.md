## Kill Conditions

A candidate dies immediately if a single competent local specialist already owns:
- identity
- compatibility
- exclusions
- supersession
- stock
- delivery
- checkout
- trust

Also kill when:
- Amazon/marketplace solves exact decision reliably
- OEM has excellent DTC + fitment + availability
- compatibility reduces to one universal SKU
- seller scarcity is actually low demand
- suppliers won't authorize/re-sell
- one fragile source controls all stock
- installation/licensing burden dominates
- returns/support destroy contribution
- exact-model intent is almost entirely support-only
- best merchant is already excellent

**A bad market is one where we can be 10% better.**

**A great market is one where the missing object literally does not exist.**
