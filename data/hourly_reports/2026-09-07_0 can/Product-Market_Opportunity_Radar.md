## Hard-rejection lessons this run

### 1. Query whitespace ≠ merchant whitespace

A market can have terrible comparison content while still having efficient retail, stock and delivery.

### 2. Product ladder ≠ margin ladder

A 3× or 8× jump between product tiers creates comparison intent, but says nothing about dealer margin.

### 3. Fast local fulfillment is a major negative-space killer

When target-market stores already deliver within 24h, the supply/logistics component of PROVEN ELSEWHERE × BROKEN HERE collapses.

### 4. Broadly distributed brands are structurally harder

Bosch Professional has abundant multi-country retail. Any specialist thesis must create value through content, bundles, service or lead generation — not merely listing the same SKU.
