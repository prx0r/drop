### Economic equations after trade pricing

`landed_cost = dealer_price + merchant_borne_freight + fulfillment`

`variable_non_ad_cost = payment_processing + expected_returns_reserve + chargebacks + variable_support + warranty_handling_reserve`

`pre_ad_contribution = net_selling_price - landed_cost - variable_non_ad_cost`

`break_even_cvr = expected_cpc / pre_ad_contribution`

`break_even_cpc = realistic_cvr × pre_ad_contribution`

`expected_profit_per_click = realistic_cvr × pre_ad_contribution - expected_cpc`

`economic_headroom = realistic_cvr × pre_ad_contribution / expected_cpc`

## 17. BAYESIAN / EVIDENCE KILL RULES

After break-even CVR is known:

Simple initial prior:
`CVR ~ Beta(1,1)`

After `o` orders from `n` qualified clicks:
`CVR | data ~ Beta(1+o, 1+n-o)`

Evaluate:
`P(actual_CVR > break_even_CVR | data)`

Operating policy:
- >80% + positive realized contribution → SCALE cautiously
- 40–80% → WAIT / gather evidence
- 15–40% → FIX only when a concrete downstream defect exists
- <15% with clean tracking and qualified traffic → PAUSE/KILL

Do not kill by calendar date alone.
