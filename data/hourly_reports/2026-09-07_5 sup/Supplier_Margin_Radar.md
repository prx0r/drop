# Supplier Margin Radar — 2026-09-06 23:00

## Executive summary

This run did not broaden into generic weather-station wholesalers. The highest-value upstream candidate is still Norway professional/prosumer Davis weather stations, and the key job was to reduce the remaining channel and fulfillment uncertainty.

The most important resolution is that **Hovdan is explicitly recruiting dealers/resellers**, not merely ordinary business customers. Its dealer page says “Become a reseller of our products,” while its B2B sales terms explicitly discuss inspection before goods are “resold.” That materially strengthens the case that a specialist ecommerce dealer can sit downstream of Hovdan, although Davis-specific approval, exact trade pricing and marketplace restrictions still need confirmation.

A second important finding is a new **stock-allocation risk**. Hovdan currently exposes a 35-product weather-station category with 29 Davis SKUs. Vantage Vue + WeatherLink Console is in stock, while the main wireless Vantage Pro2 + WeatherLink listing is currently out of stock. Marineshop independently shows the base Pro2 unavailable at both its own and supplier warehouse with unknown lead time, while the Fan-Asp variant and several accessories remain available through external stock. This suggests that base-station replenishment, not accessory supply, may become the operational bottleneck.

Current signal:
