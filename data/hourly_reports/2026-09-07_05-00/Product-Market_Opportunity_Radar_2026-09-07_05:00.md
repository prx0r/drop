# Product-Market Opportunity Radar — 2026-09-07 05:00

## Executive summary

**1 fresh candidate clears the bar, conditionally:** Finland Hunter Hydrawise irrigation controls, built as a **fast-stock upgrade/configuration specialist**, not a generic garden store.

The strongest upstream signal this hour was the Hunter X2 + WAND upgrade path versus HC/HPC/Pro-HC. Fresh target-market checks confirm that Finland has real Hunter demand and a competent specialist, but core controllers are often shown on roughly **2-week lead times**, while mature Dutch source-market specialists hold X2 and WAND inventory for immediate dispatch.

That creates a narrower but credible negative space:
**proven smart-irrigation demand × slow core-SKU availability × under-explained upgrade/compatibility choices**.

The candidate is capped at **74/100** because Finland is seasonal, one competent specialist already exists, wholesale economics are unknown, and X2/WAND has non-trivial connectivity/warranty risk.

## Top transplant opportunity

### Finland — Hunter Hydrawise upgrade/configuration specialist

**Archetype:** exact-model specialist + upgrade-path decision engine + fast local stock + sensors/accessories.

**Source-market proof:** Dutch irrigation specialist Raintechshop currently lists X2-601-E around **€125.65 incl VAT** and WAND around **€125.59 incl VAT**, both as stocked specialist products with manuals, related-product attach and immediate fulfillment.

**Target-market evidence:** Finnish specialist Järvenkylä lists:
- X2-601-E: €147.30 ex VAT ≈ **€184.86 incl 25.5% VAT**, ~2-week delivery
- WAND: €108.90 ex VAT ≈ **€136.67 incl VAT**
- HPC-401-E: €465 ex VAT ≈ **€583.58 incl VAT**, ~2-week delivery
- Rain-Clik, Soil-Clik, Roam and other ecosystem accessories

Matsco also markets Hunter irrigation products and financing in Finland, while HobbyHall carries Hunter controller/bundle variants. This is under-supplied demand, not zero-demand whitespace.

### Negative-space evidence

The strongest gap is not checkout. It is:
- X2 vs HC/HPC/Pro-HC
- when WAND is actually worth adding
- 2.4 GHz / WPA / mesh Wi-Fi compatibility
- one vs two sensor inputs
- flow-meter support
- cloud dependence
- used-controller ownership transfer
- winter shutdown and spring recommissioning
- whether a problematic X2/WAND should be upgraded rather than debugged

Recent practitioner reports in 2026 describe repeated X2/WAND connection failures and some installers preferring built-in-Wi-Fi HC/HPC units. That makes expert pre-sale routing and warranty policy part of the moat — and also a risk.
