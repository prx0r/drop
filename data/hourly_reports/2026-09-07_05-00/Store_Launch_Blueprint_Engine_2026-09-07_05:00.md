## 17. BAYESIAN / EVIDENCE KILL RULES

Start `CVR ~ Beta(1,1)`. After `o` orders from `n` qualified clicks: `Beta(1+o,1+n-o)`.

Evaluate `P(CVR > break_even_CVR | evidence)`:
- >80% + positive realized contribution -> SCALE cautiously
- 40–80% -> WAIT
- 15–40% -> FIX only with concrete defect
- <15% with clean tracking/qualified traffic -> PAUSE/KILL

No arbitrary day-based kill rule.
