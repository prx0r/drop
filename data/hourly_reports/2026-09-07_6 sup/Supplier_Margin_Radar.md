## Executive summary

This run is a material step forward because the newest Product-Market report promoted **Norway professional/prosumer weather stations — especially Davis Vantage Pro2/Vantage Vue — to an 81/100 candidate**, with supplier cost as the single highest-value unknown.

Rather than finding generic weather wholesalers, this run traced the actual Nordic channel.

The strongest actionable discovery is **Hovdan-Poly AS in Norway**. Hovdan already carries a deep Davis weather-station catalog and, separately, operates a manual B2B/dealer channel with **net B2B prices, exclusive discounts, 30-day payment terms, access to the full assortment with stock status, and normally one-working-day dispatch from stock**. This does not yet prove a profitable Davis reseller margin, but it converts the Norway weather-station opportunity from “find a supplier” into one precise validation task: **open a Hovdan dealer account and reveal the net prices for the four core Davis stations plus accessories**.

Davis itself confirms an international distributor/reseller model, exposes an “Interested in Becoming a Reseller” route, says some countries have exclusive distributors, and directs international service/repair through local resellers. Therefore the second task is to ask Davis which company controls Norway/Nordic weather distribution and whether Hovdan is an approved downstream dealer path.

A useful comparator also emerged in Finland: current AEM/Davis material names **Ilkka Lilja Oy (ILOY)** as Davis’s official Finnish importer. Sweden has a mature specialist template in **ELJI Elektronik**, which has sold Davis since 1997 and adds installation, calibration, maintenance and advice. That supports the upstream thesis that the merchant moat is not just “sell the box”; it is configuration + accessories + installation/setup + ongoing service.
