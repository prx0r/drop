# DEEP AUDIT 1 — HELIOS ELS LEGACY VENTILATION

## 1. EXECUTIVE THESIS

Potential narrow German compatibility-commerce graph for old Helios ELS bathroom/WC ventilation installations: identify the housing generation and electrical plug arrangement, map to the correct current fan insert/control variant, specify required retrofit connector where needed, then route to live German stock. The installed housing is embedded in the wall/building and can survive multiple fan generations, making component life shorter than asset life.

## 2. WHY THIS EXISTS

Helios states that it continues to offer a large number of spare parts for all ELS generations. Its spare-parts process requires exact article number, product version, production code, serial number and product designation from the type plate. This is strong evidence that revision-level identity matters.

A current specialist listing for Helios ELS-VEZ 60 explicitly distinguishes three historical housing cohorts:
- 1992–2008: works without conversion, 4-pole plug;
- 1989–1992: works without conversion, 4-pole plug;
- 1984–1989: requires an additional plug/contact component no. 20590.

That creates a valuable negative/conditional compatibility edge rather than generic replacement.

Primary/strong sources:
https://www.heliosventilatoren.de/de/service/kundendienst/ersatzteilwesen
https://haustechnik-binder.de/de/Lueftung/HELIOS-Ersatz-Ventilatoren-ELS-1984-bis-2008/HELIOS-Ventilator-ELS-VEZ-60-mit-Nachlauf-Nr.-0426

Germany × Helios × ELS under-plaster ventilation housing × 1984–2008 generations × fan insert / plug / control variant × fan failure.

## 4. LIVE EVIDENCE

A-grade OEM: Helios spare-parts page says most replacement parts are kept in stock and that parts are available across ELS generations; exact type-plate attributes are required for identification.

B-grade specialist: Haustechnik Binder exposes exact generation conditions for ELS-VEZ 60 and the additional plug required for 1984–1989 housings. Public retail price observed: €249.90 incl. VAT; delivery 1–3 working days. This is a retail offer, not our supplier cost.

## 5. COMPATIBILITY GRAPH

Known supported edges:

# DEEP-AUDIT KILLS

## Siedle legacy door-entry systems — KILL

This looked almost ideal. Siedle states that the 6+n system was standard from the 1950s through the 1990s and was installed **millions of times**. It explicitly maps eight old handset generations to the HTA 811 replacement. It also publishes Vario 511→611 adapter-based modernization and TL 411 replacement solutions. That is extraordinary installed-base/supersession depth.

But it fails the ownership-gap doctrine: Siedle itself has already built the modernization graph, offers type-specific replacement products and routes customers into its Fachhändler network. Independent sellers also publish universal 4+n handset mappings. GeoDrop would mostly repackage excellent OEM truth.

Sources:
https://www.siedle.de/de-de/home/produkte/erweiterte-moeglichkeiten/modernisierung/austausch-haustelefon/
https://www.siedle.de/de-de/home/produkte/erweiterte-moeglichkeiten/modernisierung/vario-alt-zu-vario-neu/
https://www.siedle.de/de-de/home/produkte/erweiterte-moeglichkeiten/modernisierung/austausch-tuerstationen-und-briefkaesten/

MOST IMPORTANT KILL of the run because it proves that huge installed base + deep compatibility is still insufficient when the OEM already owns migration knowledge.

## GEP / Rewatec / Mall rainwater-manager parts — KILL

This category exposes a fascinating reusable mechanism: the same underlying pumps/PCBs were private-labelled across Regent, Rainman, Juncus, Rewatec McRain, Mall Monsun, Rhebau Regenmax and other systems. Generation matters. A D-811665 board fits second/third-generation GEP Regent S and also specific Rewatec/Mall/Rhebau systems. Another RME control board has a 2002–2003 measurement-system exception. This is exactly the kind of cross-brand compatibility graph GeoDrop should seek.

However, incumbents already discovered it. regenwasser24, GEP/Dehoust specialists, all4water and Installationshandel explicitly publish cross-brand mappings, successor pumps and model exceptions. Therefore the information arbitrage is already substantially owned.
