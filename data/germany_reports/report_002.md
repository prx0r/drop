## EXECUTIVE THESIS

The UK has abundant legacy installed-base commerce, but it is also unusually well served by long-running technical spare-parts specialists. The canonical GeoDrop pattern repeatedly appeared — decades-old installed assets, production-revision differences, obsolete components, supersessions and real replacement urgency — but most attractive cells failed the ownership-gap test because a competent specialist already resolves model → part → compatibility → checkout.

The strongest **near-miss** is legacy **Johnson & Starley warm-air heating → Aquair successor migration**. It has a real UK-specific installed base, 1960s–1990s legacy generations, forced replacement/successor migration, and a high-value transaction. However, warm-air work requires specialist competence and frequently a specific Gas Safe warm-air qualification; the purchase is therefore dominated by survey/installation rather than lightweight ecommerce. This is better treated as an agent-native service/intermediation graph than a GeoDrop parts storefront.

The most instructive **hard kills** were Klargester BioDisc wastewater parts, legacy Gledhill BoilerMate parts, uPVC multipoint locks, Dometic/Seitz caravan windows, Thetford cassette toilets, Dimplex/Creda storage-heater parts and shower/enclosure spares. Each looked structurally attractive until incumbent audit revealed strong compatibility-aware merchants.

# DEEP AUDIT 1 — JOHNSON & STARLEY LEGACY WARM-AIR → AQUAIR

## Executive thesis

A UK cohort of homes built primarily in the 1960s–1990s still uses Johnson & Starley warm-air heating. Legacy heaters such as J25-32, later Hi-Spec models and Economaire units can survive for decades, but eventual replacement is complicated because owners often want to retain existing ductwork while replacing obsolete gas-heater hardware. Johnson & Starley’s Aquair hydronic air-handler range is explicitly positioned as a way to retain the warm-air distribution system while using a modern boiler.

This is commercially valuable but structurally service-heavy.

## Installed-base evidence

A specialist Johnson & Starley repair source states that the J25-32 was introduced around the 1960s, evolved through Mk2/Mk3 generations, was probably one of the most successful early central-heating warm-air heaters, and that many Mk3 examples remain in use. This is useful survival evidence but not a quantified UK installed-base count.

Source:
https://johnson-and-starley-repairs.co.uk/johnson-and-starley-j25-32.html

Evidence grade: B — first-hand specialist/operator, not OEM census.

The same specialist documents Aquair as specifically designed to replace common Johnson & Starley systems from the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s including J25-32, Hi-Spec J32 and Economaire, with multiple S-series sizes and upflow/downflow configurations.

Source:
https://www.johnson-and-starley-repairs.co.uk/aquair-combi-boiler-to-warm-air-heat-interface.html

Evidence grade: B.

## Lifecycle trigger

`legacy warm-air heater → obsolete/failing heater or gas-compliance issue → preserve existing ductwork → select compatible Aquair air handler + boiler + plenum/configuration → specialist install`

Trigger classes:
- OBSOLESCENCE
- FAILURE
- SUCCESSOR_MIGRATION

## Compatibility graph

Evidence-backed nodes/edges currently supported:

- J25-32 / Hi-Spec J32 / Economaire → candidate replacement family: Aquair
- legacy duct distribution → retained when suitable
- Aquair S-10 / S-16 / S-20 / S-25 / S-36 → different output classes
- upflow vs downflow → installation-layout condition
- plenum/adaptor choice → depends on legacy cabinet/duct arrangement
- Aquair requires a separate hot-water heat source such as compatible boiler

Unresolved edges:
- exact old-model-to-Aquair-size matrix
- plenum requirements per model/cabinet
- whether every historical generation has an approved direct migration
- exact survey rules

# DEEP AUDIT 2 — KLARGESTER BIODISC LEGACY DRIVE COMPONENTS

## Why it triggered

Klargester states more than 70 years of UK wastewater experience, and the BioDisc has a 50-year product history. The product family contains durable installed treatment plants with externally replaceable motors, gearboxes, belts, pulleys, bearings, capacitors and alarms.

Sources:
https://www.klargester.co.uk/
https://www.tanks-direct.co.uk/waste-water-tanks/sewage-treatment-plant/klargester-sewage-treatment-plants/c1042

There is genuine revision complexity. For BA/BA-X/BB systems, replacement motor/gearbox requirements differ depending on old motor type and date. ECS Pumps states that replacing a Vipa gearbox from before January 2013 requires an external capacitor box; October 2010–January 2013 units may also need it, whereas Panasonic-version replacement has a changed capacitor supplied with the newer unit.

Source:
https://ecspumps.co.uk/product/ba-bb-panasonic-gearbox-motor/

Tanks Direct explicitly distinguishes BA BioDisc belts pre-2006 versus post-2006.

Source:
https://tanks-direct.co.uk/products/klargester-ba-biodisc-sewage-treatment-plant-up-to-6pe

This is excellent GeoDrop-shaped compatibility data.

## Why it dies

Direct Drainage already stocks a broad BioDisc-specific catalog and explicitly separates:
- BA/BA-X/BB motor/gearboxes
- BC/BD/BE Bauer gearboxes
- BF and BG units
- pre/post-generation drive components

Sources:
https://direct-drainage.co.uk/collections/klargester-biodisc-spare-parts
https://direct-drainage.co.uk/collections/motors-gearboxes/motor-gearboxes

It provides same-day dispatch for in-stock spare parts and direct technical contact. Tanks Direct also exposes model-specific spares and generation distinctions.

# DEEP AUDIT 3 — LEGACY uPVC MULTIPOINT DOOR LOCKS

## Why it triggered

Old GU, Yale, Cego/Zenith/Bowater and other multipoint door systems create a classic identification problem:
- brand stamped on faceplate
- locking-point pattern
- PZ/centres
- backset
- spindle type
- operation type
- old vs new gearbox
- gearbox-only vs complete-strip replacement

Some old operating modes are no longer supported. UPVC Store notes, for example, that its old-style GU replacement gearbox is lift-lever only; split-spindle and fast-locking versions require a new full-length multipoint lock.

Source:
https://www.upvc-store.co.uk/products/gu-old-style-40mm-replacement-multipoint-door-lock-gearbox-lift-lever-operation

JCP Hardware explicitly markets a replacement Cego/Surelock gearbox for discontinued Cego/Zenith/Bowater systems.

Source:
https://jcphardware.co.uk/cego-surelock-gearbox-door-lock-centre-case-twin-spindle-35mm-backset/

## Why it dies

The incumbent layer is already compatibility-aware. UPVC Door Locks provides an identification workflow based on manufacturer, locking points, backset and PZ. UPVC Hardware similarly explains model identification and exact fitment. Multiple merchants transact the old-style gearboxes directly.

Sources:

https://www.upvc-hardware.co.uk/shop/locks-gearing/multipoint-upvc-door-locks/yale-upvc-door-locks/yale-g2000-lock-case-gearbox/
https://www.upvc-hardware.co.uk/shop/locks-gearing/multipoint-upvc-door-locks/cego-upvc-door-locks/cego-upvc-door-lockcase-old-type/

### Decision

**BEST NEAR-MISS:** Johnson & Starley legacy warm-air → Aquair successor migration, but as a service/qualification graph rather than conventional ecommerce.

**MOST IMPORTANT KILL:** shower-enclosure replacement rollers/parts. It initially looks like perfect visual-identification commerce, but incumbents already accept photos, identify the exact component and return a purchase link. That is precisely the ownership layer GeoDrop would have built.

**NEW REUSABLE MARKET MECHANISM:** The UK is sufficiently mature in legacy repair ecommerce that the useful negative signal is not “many old systems”; it is whether compatibility truth remains separated from transaction. Where specialist merchants already join them, kill immediately. Where selection requires a regulated/specialist survey, route the atom into service-market infrastructure instead of forcing it into product ecommerce.

**HIGHEST-EVI NEXT RESEARCH ACTION:** On the next UK run, move away from mature heating/plumbing/caravan/shower/door-hardware spare ecosystems and deliberately scan less-digitised installed bases in light-commercial, agricultural, building-control, water-management, specialist appliance and small industrial categories. The required discovery signal should be: OEM/PDF compatibility truth + current local stock + no photo/measurement/model finder + no excellent specialist checkout.

No paid-ad probe is justified from this run.
