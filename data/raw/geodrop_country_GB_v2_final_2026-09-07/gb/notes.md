# United Kingdom GeoDrop v2 reference notes

- Canonical country code is **GB** (ISO alpha-2); human alias UK is retained.
- This is a **fresh standalone country research run**, not cumulative with Norway/Finland.
- The UK is mature/high-competition. Large ecommerce penetration and installed bases are treated as structural priors only, not as opportunity scores.
- Geographic definitions are deliberately preserved: some sources cover UK, some Great Britain, some England. No silent denominator flattening.
- Publicly observable data is populated; authenticated Keyword Planner metrics, dealer net costs/reseller rights and complete merchant censuses remain `MISSING_REQUIRED`.
- Initial scores are exploratory reference snapshots and **not backtest eligible**. Prospective runs must freeze candidate universe, strategy version, evidence availability and predictions before outcomes.
- Negative evidence is a feature: generic plug-in solar kits, Tesla Model Y specialist stores and generic heat-pump controls are downgraded/killed where incumbent evidence is already strong.
