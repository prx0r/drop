# Sweden GeoDrop Country Intelligence v2 — Research Notes

Sweden is intentionally modeled as both a target country and a **mature source-market oracle**. Large installed bases are not automatically opportunities. The bootstrap explicitly preserves high-quality negative controls: generic heat-pump parts, smart-meter readers, holiday-home remote monitors, radon monitors and Tesla Model Y accessories.

The strongest unresolved frontiers are (1) solar lifecycle/retrofit exact-model cells as new-install growth slows against a 314,600-system installed base, and (2) emerging non-Tesla EV model cohorts where visible source-market assortment may temporarily outrun Swedish specialist catalog depth.
