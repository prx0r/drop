# Agent Prompt — Supplier Due Diligence

For each anchor SKU obtain or explicitly mark unknown: net price, VAT basis, MOQ/MOV, freight matrix, warehouse, stock and replenishment, ecommerce/reseller permission, MAP, blind/direct shipping, stock/product feed, image/content rights, payment terms, returns, DOA, warranty/RMA, service responsibilities and geographic restrictions.

Verify manufacturer authorization independently. Public scarcity never proves supply scarcity. Store evidence and date. A reseller-rights unknown blocks commerce launch.
