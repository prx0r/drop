# Agent Prompt — Discover Product × Country Cells

Use only the country pack and fresh corroborating research. Start from structural ecosystems and established source-market products, not novelty-product lists.

For each cell answer:
- What exact problem/installed base creates demand?
- What exact product/SKU solves it?
- Which mature market proves the merchant/service model?
- What native queries indicate intent?
- How many local sellers are actually good?
- Is the gap merchant availability or merely content?
- What authorized supplier path can serve the market?
- What fact would kill this fastest?

Create `product_markets.jsonl` rows in `DISCOVERED` or `HOLD_VALIDATE`; never skip directly to launch.
