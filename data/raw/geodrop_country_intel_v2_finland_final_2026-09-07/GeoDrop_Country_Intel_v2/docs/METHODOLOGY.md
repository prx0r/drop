# Research Methodology

GeoDrop starts from populations of owned systems and persistent jobs, not from supplier catalogues.

## Discovery loop
1. Enumerate country-specific installed bases from official/industry sources.
2. Normalize definitions/cohorts/geography and calculate only defensible growth/replacement signals.
3. Find observed failure/maintenance/replacement/compliance problems from insurers, regulators, service bodies, forums and search behavior.
4. Generate native query forests from first-party terminology and mature-market merchant/catalogue vocabulary; measure with Google Ads/Trends and SERP/Shopping data.
5. Census target merchants, including hidden distributors/installers/B2B channels.
6. Identify mature adjacent source markets and model their assortment/service/content depth.
7. Quantify source-target lag.
8. Promote only high-coverage ecosystems to exact SKU/supplier economics.
9. Run narrow free/paid probes; capture outcomes.
10. Learn which frozen strategy classes predict profitable probes.

## Discovery principle
A market can be interesting without a current launch candidate. Do not force ecosystem evidence into a product recommendation.
