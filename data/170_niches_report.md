# High-Certainty Agentic Commerce — Installed-Base Niche Hunt

**Date:** 7 Sep 2026  
**Purpose:** Find neglected, locally stocked, compatibility-heavy installed-base niches where supply is operationally competent but digitally weak enough for a visual + technical + compatibility graph to become the definitive retail frontend.

## Executive result

The strongest hunting ground is **long-lived building systems + leisure equipment**. The recurring pattern is:

`OLD INSTALLED ASSET → owner sees physical part/problem → model/generation ambiguity → OEM/distributor has stock and diagrams → consumer buying surface is trade-oriented / fragmented / manual → wrong-part cost is high → parcel-compatible replacement exists.`

The key filter is **not seller count**. It is: **can we materially beat the best existing specialist on certainty?**

### Evidence labels
- **A — ATTACK:** current evidence suggests real structural fit + plausible merchant gap.
- **B — VERIFY:** very strong compatibility economics, but supplier economics / best-specialist quality still need checking.
- **C — SCAN:** promising mutation for the discovery engine; not yet live-validated.
- **BENCHMARK:** compatibility problem is real but current specialist/OEM digital quality is already strong.

# Supplier Digital Arbitrage gate — do not launch without all 8

For every niche, require:

1. **supplier stock access** — API/CSV/portal/scrape/manual feed possible
2. **stable supplier SKU / MPN**
3. **wholesale or referral economics**
4. **direct/blind shipping OR clear click-out/affiliate path**
5. **dispatch SLA**
6. **returns/RMA process**
7. **stock freshness**
8. **consumer-safe / legally valid sales route**

The highest-information next query is not more TAM research. It is: **who will give us these parts, at what net price, with what stock feed, and who owns returns?**

# Canonical compatibility atom

The discovery engine should score at **asset × generation × component**, not category or even model alone:

`brand × installed_asset_model × production_generation/serial cohort × subcomponent × revision × supersession × electrical/physical interface × geography`

Examples:
- Astral CTX × Series 1/pre-Aug-2014 × seal plate
- Itho CVE ECO RFT × SP × pre-week-48/2012 × service module
- Dolphin M-series × generation × power-supply family
- Danfoss floor heat × controller generation × 24/230V × RA/M30 connection
- Allaway old central unit × wall-inlet/cover generation × new hose/receiver

This is why generic ecommerce data is structurally inadequate.

---

# Best-specialist falsifier

Before greenlighting a campaign, compare us to the **single best current specialist** on:

- exact model identification
- photo/type-plate intake
- production generation
- supersession graph
- explicit incompatible models
- technical attributes
- real local stock
- manuals/diagrams
- install/safety constraints
- successor/retrofit routing
- Q&A coverage
- Merchant Center/JSON-LD feed quality

If a specialist already scores ~9/10, **kill or narrow the niche**. This killed Mitsubishi remotes as the first Finnish wedge and moves Automower, Swegon CASA, Itho generic parts, mainstream Australian salt cells/spa controls and Jura into benchmark status.

# Final prioritization

**Country order for this thesis today:**

1. **Finland** — best concentration of long-lived building hardware + strong distributors + local language + installed-base density.
