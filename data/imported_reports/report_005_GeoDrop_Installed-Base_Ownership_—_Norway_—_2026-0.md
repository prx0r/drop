# 2. STRONGEST CANDIDATE — NOBØ LEGACY HEATING CONTROL

## 2.1 Executive thesis

Norway has a deep installed base of Nobø electric heating products, with the company stating that it has sold more than 20 million panel heaters globally and has over 100 years of electric-heating history. Historic Norwegian market evidence also shows Nobø/Glen Dimplex as a dominant professional panel-heater supplier. This does not quantify Orion 700 installs specifically, but it validates the scale of the parent installed asset ecosystem.

The commercial atom is narrower:

**Norway × Nobø × Orion 700 / Series 8 R80 / legacy floor thermostats × control failure or migration × receiver/thermostat/hub × firmware and device compatibility uncertainty.**

Primary/current sources:
https://www.nobo.no/produkter
https://www.nobo.no/om-oss
https://www.nobo.no/faq

Historical market context:
https://www.bygg.no/a/2100276

## 2.2 Why this exists

Orion 700 was marketed in Norway by at least 2008 as a radio-based central controller for panel heaters and other electrical devices, including cabin remote-heating use through GSM/internet services.

Source:
https://www.aftenposten.no/norge/i/nw7pa/noboe-orion-700

Nobø currently says:
- all units compatible with Orion 700 are also compatible with Nobø HUB / Energy Control;
- new panel heaters and thermostats can work with an old Orion 700 **only if Orion firmware is the latest version SW04.00**;
- older floor thermostats depend on the exact type and require support review.

This is real compatibility logic, not generic product selection.

Primary source:
https://www.nobo.no/faq
