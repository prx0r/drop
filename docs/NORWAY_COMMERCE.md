# Norway Commerce Intelligence

*Key data points for opportunity evaluation. Updated 2026-09-06.*

---

## Market Overview

- **E-commerce market size:** $10.35B (2026), projected $14.84B by 2031 (7.48% CAGR)
- **Online shoppers (last 30 days):** 86%
- **Cross-border shoppers (last year):** 78%
- **Sweden replaced China** as most popular cross-border source for Norwegians
- **80% of population in urban areas** — easy last-mile delivery

## Consumer Behavior

- **86% bought online in last 30 days** (highest in Nordics)
- **78% bought internationally last year**
- **87% expect delivery within 5 days**
- **Males shop online more than females** (trend expected to continue)
- **Second-hand selling is popular** — more Norwegians sell than buy used

## Payment

- **Vipps:** 4.6M users / 5.5M population — **must support**
- Online bank payments preferred by 32%
- 61% consider ease of payment important
- 10% said they wouldn't have abandoned cart had Vipps been available

## Prisjakt — Our Secret Weapon

- Shows all stores and prices for each product, including stores that don't pay
- Offers sorted by price
- 2M monthly visitors
- For any model SKU we can ask:
  - How many sellers?
  - Which sellers?
  - Price spread?
  - Which look specialist?
  - Which have stock?
  - Shipping?
  - Reviews?
  - How good are product pages?
  - Has seller count changed?

## Cross-Border VAT

- **VOEC:** Simplified VAT for goods ≤2,999 NOK
- Foreign businesses must collect Norwegian VAT from first sale
- VOEC number attached to parcel = exempt from customs clearance
- VAT rate: 25% on most goods

## Logistics

- World Bank Logistics Performance Index: high ranking
- 80% urban population = easy delivery
- Service points are most common delivery method
- Young consumers cancel if delivery method doesn't suit them

## Opportunity Signals

- **Electronics vertical specialization** generates higher AOV and repeat loyalty
- **Category expertise** beats generalist marketplaces
- **Vipps + BNPL** at checkout reduces abandonment
- **Privacy-first** approaches attract premium advertisers
- **DHL activated DFN fulfilment in Oslo (2026)** — faster local delivery for cross-border merchants

## Competition Gaps to Investigate

1. **Specialist coffee equipment** — popular in UK/US, weak specialist retailers in Norway
2. **Robot lawn mowers** — high demand, complex specifications, weak local expertise
3. **Standing desk converters** — growing category, specification-heavy
4. **Cold plunge tubs** — trending, high ticket, weak specialist retailers
5. **Ergonomic pet equipment** — growing market, emotional purchase
6. **Smart home specialist** — Komplett/Elkjøp are generalists, not specialists
