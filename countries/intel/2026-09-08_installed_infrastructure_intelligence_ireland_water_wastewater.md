## Product #2: WellDoctor.ie

Upload water lab report → extract chemistry → transform to equipment recommendation.

Symptoms:
- brown/orange staining → iron
- black staining → manganese
- rotten egg smell → H₂S
- blue/green pipe staining → low pH / corrosion
- white scale → hardness
- cloudy/gritty water → sediment
- bacteria result → disinfection problem
- low pressure → pump / pressure vessel / blockage

**The lab report is the compatibility key.** Exceptionally AI-friendly.
