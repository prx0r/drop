## Core Thesis

> **Do not chase the rapidly growing product. Chase the maintenance graph created by the installed base.**

The installation wave is visible and competitive. The **5–15 years of failures, replacements, compatibility problems, upgrades, consumables and local service searches behind it** are much less efficiently served.

## The Maintenance Graph

```text
PURCHASE → INSTALL → CONFIGURE → INTEGRATE → MAINTAIN → CONSUMABLES →
FAULT → DIAGNOSE → REPAIR → PARTS → UPGRADE → REPLACE → REMOVE
```

**The mistake:** optimizing for PURCHASE + INSTALL.
**The opportunity:** everything BELOW them.

## The Scoring Formula

For every `country × ecosystem × product/model × lifecycle problem × geography`:

```
Opportunity =
  InstalledBase
  × Aging
  × FailureFrequency
  × ProblemUrgency
  × CustomerValue
  × SearchIntent
  × SupplierFragmentation
  ÷ SERPStrength
```

Plus bonuses:
- Recurring consumable
- Safety/regulatory complexity
- Expensive local labour
- Incompatibility complexity
- High cost of downtime
- Identifiable model number
- Official installer registry
- Easily shippable component
- Rich homeowners
