## Legal Requirements

### Business Registration
- Requires Norwegian business address (or use agent)
- MVA registration for sales > NOK 50,000/year
- Consumer purchasing act applies

### Product Compliance
- CE marking required for electronics
- Norwegian safety standards (NSE)
- Energy labeling for appliances
- RoHS compliance for electronics

### Consumer Protection
- 14-day right of withdrawal
- 2-year warranty (minimum)
- Price marketing act (prisopplysningsloven)
- Return shipping: seller pays if defective
