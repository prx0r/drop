## Peer review of the 11 current campaigns

The latest commit added ten campaign files around Vallox, cabin water, heat pumps, Automower, Harvia, EV aftersales, radon, lead pipes, pipe inspection and marine pumps. Campaign #1 is actually stored as `GOLD_CAMPAIGN.md`, rather than `GOLD_CAMPAIGN_01_ALLAWAY.md`.

The core methodology is very close, but there is one major mathematical bug: the methodology describes a **multiplicative score divided by OEM quality, best-specialist quality, return risk and safety risk**, but the actual campaign scores simply sum 13 positive 1–10 scores to produce `/130`. The denominator penalties are not being applied.

That explains anomalies such as Automower receiving 105/130 even though its own campaign admits Husqvarna and specialists already publish good compatibility information. Husqvarna's current catalog indeed exposes exact model fitment across 1A, 1.3A, 2.2A, 4.2A and 7A power supplies. ([Husqvarna][1])

I would replace the current score with:

```
HCC_V2 =

25% information rent
    identity difficulty
    compatibility complexity
    supersession complexity

15% installed-base economics
    installed units
    replacement frequency
    urgency

20% supplier arbitrage
    stock depth
    operational quality
    obtainable net price
    stock feed
    direct ship / fulfilment

15% distribution fit
    parcel suitability
    GMC eligibility
    visual search suitability
    structured-data potential

15% economics
    contribution/order
    AOV

    actual supplier evidence
    actual merchant-gap evidence

MINUS:
0–15 best-specialist penalty

```

And before scoring, four **hard gates**:

```
SUPPLIER EXISTS AND WILL SELL TO US
STOCK CAN BE KEPT FRESH
COMPATIBILITY CAN BE PROVEN, NOT GUESSED
LEGAL/INSTALLATION RESPONSIBILITY IS EXPLICIT
```

Margins such as "25–35%" should currently be marked **UNKNOWN**, not scored. No campaign has earned a margin score until a distributor gives us actual net prices. The same applies to the break-even numbers: "40 orders/month" means nothing unless fixed monthly cost and CAC are stated.

## The single strongest portfolio insight

The campaign research is converging onto **three variants of the same economic primitive**:

```
A. REPLACEMENT
broken thing → identify → exact compatible part

B. MIGRATION
obsolete thing → identify current state → exact successor bundle

C. REPAIR ORCHESTRATION
failed thing → identify → decide repair vs replace → part + technician
```

Allaway is **A**.

Ouman and Danfoss are **B**.

EV charger PCBs are **C**.

And all three use the same underlying kernel:

```
PHYSICAL ASSET
    ↓
IDENTITY
    ↓
STATE / GENERATION
```
